About Pawsome Shelter

For over a decade, we have been the sanctuary of choice for abandoned and rescued hounds. Our mission is to bridge the gap between shelter life and forever families through empathy, training, and education.

We believe every dog has a unique personality and deserves a bespoke adoption match. Our facility is designed to reduce stress and maximize socialization, ensuring our dogs are ready for their new homes.
